# Coaching-piano apps: how they coach & retain — survey

_Research for [issue #5](https://github.com/niclaeysthomas-ctrl/piano-dojo/issues/5) · part of the [Piano Dojo → free-accompanist map (#1)](https://github.com/niclaeysthomas-ctrl/piano-dojo/issues/1)._

_Status: work in progress — skeleton committed early to guard against infra loss. Full findings to follow._

## Scope

Survey Simply Piano, Flowkey, PianoGroove, Melodics, Yousician (+ a notable jazz/chords tool) on:
feedback loop (mic / MIDI / none, and chord-level vs single-note), curriculum structure & sequencing,
long-term retention mechanics, and chord/voicing/improv coverage. Then synthesize what to steal / avoid
for a 2-year jazz free-accompaniment coach whose principle is
"fully useful hearing nothing; MIDI optional power-up; mic never trusted for chords."
