# Coaching-piano apps: how they coach & retain — survey

_Research for [issue #5](https://github.com/niclaeysthomas-ctrl/piano-dojo/issues/5) · part of the [Piano Dojo → free-accompanist map (#1)](https://github.com/niclaeysthomas-ctrl/piano-dojo/issues/1)._

Surveys Simply Piano, flowkey, Yousician, Melodics, PianoGroove, and iReal Pro (the jazz tool) on four axes: **feedback loop** (mic / MIDI / none, and chord-level vs single-note), **curriculum structure**, **long-term retention mechanics**, and **chord/voicing/improv coverage**. Then synthesizes what to steal and avoid for a 2-year jazz free-accompaniment coach whose principle is *"fully useful hearing nothing; MIDI optional power-up; mic never trusted for chords."* Claims are attributed inline; where a fact comes only from marketing vs. official support docs, that is flagged, because the gap is itself the finding.

## Quick comparison

| App | Feedback loop | Chord-level? | Curriculum | Retention | Jazz/voicing depth |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simply Piano | Mic default, MIDI optional | Marketing yes; **support docs hedge** | 33+ courses, explicit **2-yr** path | Streaks, points, levels, challenges | Low (pop/classical) |
| flowkey | Mic default, MIDI/BT optional | Marketing yes; support docs silent | Song-first courses | Light gamification | Low–moderate |
| Yousician | **Polyphonic mic** or MIDI | Yes via mic, **degrades** on chords | Missions/paths, 1500+ items | Weekly challenges, streaks, adaptive | Low |
| Melodics | **MIDI only, no mic** | Yes (MIDI = exact) | Courses + drill Exercises | 5-min goal, streaks, trophies, flow | Moderate (groove comping) |
| PianoGroove | **None** (video + visual ref) | n/a | 40 courses, syllabuses | Membership + community + live | **Deep** (the model) |
| iReal Pro | **None** (it's the band) | n/a | Chord-chart library | Utility standard + shared charts | Accompaniment engine |

## Simply Piano (Simply / ex-JoyTunes)

**Feedback.** Microphone by default — the app "listens to your playing through your device's microphone and tells you, note by note, whether you hit the right key." MIDI (USB) is the optional upgrade giving "perfect note recognition," recommended by their [help center](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/6210608-ways-to-improve-note-recognition-in-simply-piano-for-ios-keyboard-electronic-piano) "particularly when you get to playing more advanced music." Note: the official support article does **not** claim chord recognition; the "chords work in a quiet room" claim comes from third-party reviews ([digitalpiano.com guide](https://www.digitalpiano.com/dk/en/blog/post/guide-to-simply-piano/)), and the doc's own hedge ("more advanced music" → MIDI) implies mic reliability drops with complexity.

**Curriculum.** 33+ courses on a shared foundation (finger numbers, staff reading, clefs) that then forks into parallel paths — e.g. a Soloist Pathway (sheet music, sight-reading, technique, RH melody + LH accompaniment). The full curriculum is officially designed to take **two years**; songs ship in simplified→advanced arrangements that unlock with skill ([pianodreamers review](https://www.pianodreamers.com/simply-piano-review/)).

**Retention.** Textbook gamification: streaks, points scored on accuracy+timing, achievements, levels, and challenges forming a reinforcement loop, plus difficulty adaptation ([Trophy case study](https://www.trophy.so/blog/simply-piano-gamification-case-study)).

**Jazz/voicing.** Minimal — mainstream pop/classical with basic LH accompaniment; no voicings/reharm/improv.

## flowkey

**Feedback.** Built-in device microphone, no cables; MIDI or Bluetooth optional "to enhance accuracy even more." Its signature **Wait Mode** "waits for you to hit the right notes" before advancing. Marketing says it detects "notes and chords," but the official [acoustic-piano help article](https://help.flowkey.com/en/articles/651003-can-i-use-an-acoustic-piano-with-flowkey) is silent on chords and instead lists mic caveats (limit background noise, tune to standard pitch), and [flowkey](https://www.flowkey.com/en) recommends MIDI for "complex or fast-paced songs, where precise note detection is vital."

**Curriculum.** Song-first: learn real pieces early, hands separate, adjustable speed/loop. Courses run reading → technique → chords → chord progressions, finger crossovers, time signatures, accompaniment patterns, and improvisation.

**Retention.** Lighter on gamification than Yousician/Simply — driven by a large song library and course progression rather than streak mechanics.

**Jazz/voicing.** Teaches chords, accompaniment patterns, and some improvisation, but not jazz-specialized.

## Yousician

**Feedback.** Microphone with the most aggressive **polyphonic** engine of the group — "advanced sound recognition technology... each note and chord you play" ([Yousician blog](https://yousician.com/blog/piano-app)) — or MIDI for near-perfect accuracy. Independent testing notes the mic "struggles with digital pianos at low volume, ambient noise, and chords played with overlapping notes" ([pianistscompass](https://pianistscompass.org/reviews/apps/yousician-piano/)). Game-like falling-note highway with real-time timing feedback.

**Curriculum.** A learning path of **missions** (grouped songs, video tutorials, trainers) across Classical, Pop, and Knowledge (theory) themes — 9 levels each in Classical/Pop, 1500+ missions/exercises, with AI adjusting exercise difficulty to performance.

**Retention.** Most game-forward: weekly challenges with a competitive/social dimension, a 30-day challenge, streaks, goals, and adaptive difficulty.

**Jazz/voicing.** Broad genre coverage but no jazz voicing/reharm depth.

## Melodics

**Feedback.** **MIDI hardware only — no microphone at all**: "Melodics works best with hardware... MIDI keyboard, pad controller, or MIDI drum kit" ([how-it-works](https://melodics.com/how-it-works)). Because input is MIDI, chord/timing detection is exact. Real-time accuracy readout plus light-up keys/pads; notes color by timing (green on-time, orange early, purple late, red miss); post-performance breakdown and a Review & Repeat playback.

**Curriculum.** Courses group lessons by concept/technique/theme; separate repeatable **Exercises** drill muscle memory (scales, intervals, patterns). Producer-oriented styles (hip hop, house, funk, lo-fi) with a falling-notes interface.

**Retention.** Cleanly designed habit loop: a **5-minute Daily Goal** ("shorter, regular sessions are more effective"), streaks, trophies, and a **Flow State** system that "keeps you at the learning edge" via dynamic difficulty, plus analytics/badges.

**Jazz/voicing.** Moderate — real chord/comping and bassline work with strong rhythmic-feel emphasis, but groove-genre rather than jazz-theory depth.

## PianoGroove (the jazz specialist)

**Feedback.** **None** — no note detection. Video lessons with an on-screen interactive keyboard as *visual reference*, PDF resources, practice guides, a community forum, and live seminars ([jazz-piano-lessons](https://www.pianogroove.com/jazz-piano-lessons/)).

**Curriculum.** 40 courses / 450+ video lessons across jazz, blues, gospel, and Brazilian, organized into [syllabuses/roadmaps](https://www.pianogroove.com/syllabuses/) — e.g. beginners study Jazz Foundations + Beginner Arrangements + Extended Chords + a Hand Independence syllabus together. Foundations cover scales, intervals, triads, 7th chords, the major **ii–V–I (251)**, and playing standards.

**Retention.** No gamification — retained by **content depth, a paid membership (monthly/annual/lifetime), community, and live events**. Proof that a no-feedback, reference-plus-community model sustains serious adult learners for years.

**Jazz/voicing.** **Deep, and the closest model to the target domain:** shell/rootless voicings, extended chords, 251s, full arrangements, reharmonization, improvisation, comping, hand independence.

## iReal Pro (the accompaniment engine)

**Feedback.** **None on your playing** — it is the *band*, not the coach. A realistic backing group plus a huge chord-chart library ([irealpro.com](https://www.irealpro.com/)).

**Features.** 50 accompaniment styles (swing, bossa, blues, funk, latin, gypsy jazz…), customizable instrumentation (acoustic/electric piano, Rhodes, bass, drums, vibes, organ), adjustable tempo/key, looping; thousands of user-shared charts; tap a chord to see fingerings and **scale suggestions for improvisation**; export audio/MIDI/PDF/MusicXML.

**Jazz/voicing.** Chord-chart-centric with scale hints, but no voicing instruction or feedback — it's the natural practice habitat for free accompaniment, not a teacher of it.

## Synthesis — for a 2-year jazz free-accompaniment coach

**Steal**

- **PianoGroove's content model is the spine.** It proves the target skill set — shell/rootless voicings, 251s, reharm, arrangements, comping, hand independence — retains adults *for years with zero playing-feedback*, on depth + syllabus + community alone. Adopt its sequenced roadmaps (bundled parallel courses) and its jazz curriculum ordering.
- **A 2-year, "don't overthink what's next" structured path** (Simply Piano ships exactly this) — an explicit multi-year spine with a clear next action beats a pile of à-la-carte lessons.
- **iReal Pro-style accompaniment engine as the practice partner.** A play-along band + chord charts, adjustable tempo/key, looping, chord-symbol-centric — this *is* "fully useful hearing nothing": value lives in what it plays back, not in grading you. Comp against the band; the app never needs to hear you.
- **Melodics' habit loop and its MIDI-power-up done right.** Steal the 5-min Daily Goal, streaks, trophies, Flow-State adaptivity, and Review & Repeat. Melodics also shows the correct role for MIDI: when hardware is present, give *precise* timing/accuracy feedback and light-up cues — reserve real grading for MIDI, exactly the "optional power-up" stance.
- **flowkey's Wait Mode idea, decoupled from audio** — a self-paced "advance when you've got it" gate, driven by self-report or reference-audio call-and-response, not by trusting mic detection.

**Avoid**

- **Trusting the mic for chords — the survey validates the principle outright.** *Every* mic app hedges precisely where jazz lives: Simply routes "advanced music" to MIDI; flowkey routes "complex/fast songs" to MIDI and warns on noise/tuning; Yousician's own reviewers say the mic "struggles with... chords played with overlapping notes." Dense comping voicings are the worst case for polyphonic mic pitch-detection. Do **not** build the core loop on it.
- **Note-matching scoring as the success metric.** The Simply/Yousician "right note at the right time" grader is *hostile to improvisation*: it would punish valid reharmonization, substitution, voicing choice, and rhythmic space — the whole point of free accompaniment. If any grading exists (MIDI-only), score feel/voice-leading/harmonic fit, never note-for-note conformance.
- **The falling-notes highway as the primary mode.** It trains reaction to a scrolling target, not reading changes and voicing them from a lead sheet — the actual free-accompaniment skill. Make **chord-symbol / lead-sheet reading** (iReal/PianoGroove) the spine; reserve falling-notes for isolated drills.
- **Gating core value behind hardware.** Melodics is MIDI-only; our principle is the inverse. MIDI must be strictly additive — the app has to be fully useful with nothing plugged in and no mic.
- **Content-unlock treadmills and paywalled arrangements** (Simply's simplified→advanced unlock + upsell habit) — don't dress up content-gating as progression.
